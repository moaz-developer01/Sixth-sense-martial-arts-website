# SEO, AEO, and FEO Standards

## SEO: search engine optimization

- Give every page one search intent, a unique title, a useful meta description, and a self-referencing canonical URL.
- Use one descriptive H1, logical H2/H3 headings, short paragraphs, descriptive links, and clean URLs.
- Add relevant `SportsActivityLocation`, `LocalBusiness`, `Person`, `Course`, or `FAQPage` JSON-LD only when the facts are true and visible on the page.
- Keep location, phone, email, opening hours, and business name consistent everywhere.
- Optimize images with meaningful filenames, dimensions, compression, and accurate alt text. Never use keyword-stuffed alt text.
- Keep pages fast, keyboard accessible, mobile friendly, and free of layout shift.

## AEO: answer engine optimization

- Answer likely visitor questions directly near the top of each relevant page.
- Use clear question headings such as “What should I bring to my first class?” followed by concise, complete answers.
- Include facts that can be quoted accurately: class length, age groups, experience level, pricing approach, location, and booking steps.
- Build an FAQ from real customer questions. Keep the visible FAQ and any FAQ structured data identical.
- Cite or link to authoritative sources for health, safety, and legal claims.

## FEO: frictionless experience optimization

- Put the next action in the first viewport: call, message, book a trial, or view the schedule.
- Make contact details tap-friendly and available without forcing a form submission.
- Explain what happens after every conversion action and provide a low-friction alternative.
- Use descriptive button labels, visible focus states, accessible form errors, and no autoplay media.
- Test the complete path on a phone with slow network conditions before launch.

## Content quality gate

Before publishing, confirm that every factual claim is supplied by the academy, every location detail is verified, and each page helps a visitor make a decision. Do not invent instructors, reviews, credentials, prices, addresses, or schedules.
